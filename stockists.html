For publishers & licensors

Rights & licensing.

SouthStudio manages all rights to its catalogue directly. If you're a publisher, broadcaster, or producer interested in the following, get in touch — we're open to the right conversations.

01

Translation rights

Timeline was written in English and launched first in English and Dutch. We welcome enquiries from publishers interested in bringing it to other languages — especially where a love of logic puzzles runs deep.

02

Licensing & adaptation

The world of the Time Correction Agency, the cases, and the SouthStudio catalogue are open to discussion — games, digital adaptations, editorial use. We consider each proposal on its own merits.

03

Press & media

Review copies, interview requests, and editorial features are welcome. Write to us with your publication or channel, your deadline, and what you need. We keep it simple.
