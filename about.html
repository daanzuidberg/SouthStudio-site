The Studio

An independent studio
for the long way round.

SouthStudio creates with intention. Timeless in design, meaningful in use, and made to last.

The mission

Things worth
thinking about.

Four words everything else answers to. If a product, a sentence, or a decision doesn't serve them, it's wrong.

SouthStudio is the sensibility behind games, books, and objects — not a puzzle press, an app shop, or a publisher. Those are only formats. The studio is the quiet mark that tells you a thing was made with care, a point of view, and respect for the person on the other end.

Made by someone, not assembled by a committee. That is the whole point.

Est. 2026 · Independent

Founded in the Netherlands. Built for anyone in the world who likes to use their mind.

What we make

We make. We don't.

We make

✓Books and games built on fair deduction
✓Short films about strange questions
✓Experiences built for the screen, not ported to it
✓Objects and ideas worth keeping

We don't

✕Throwaway apps that mine attention
✕Trivia that rewards memory over reasoning
✕Streaks, timers, nags, dark patterns
✕Things you use once and discard
The standard

Five things every
release must be.

Before anything leaves the studio, it has to pass all five. If it fails one, it isn't ready, whatever the format.

01

Honest

There is always a fair path through. Nothing cheap, nothing arbitrary, the answer is earned.

02

Beautiful

Typography, materials, and detail considered to the edge. Worth keeping on a shelf.

03

Deeper than it looks

A world underneath, not just a mechanic. It rewards a second and third visit.

04

Made to last

Physical or digital, it is built to be returned to, not consumed and forgotten.

05

Respectful

Of your time, your attention, and your intelligence. We talk to a peer.
