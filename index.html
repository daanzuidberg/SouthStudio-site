The studio Why we make things

Give your attention something worthwhile to do.

Most things made for a screen are built to take attention. We are interested in the opposite: things that reward it. A puzzle that is fair. A book that stays on the shelf. A film that leaves you with a better question than it found you with.

SouthStudio is small on purpose. We make one thing at a time, finish it properly, and then start the next.

About the studio →
I.

Made slowly

Every case, page and frame is tested by a person before it leaves the studio. If it isn't fair, it doesn't ship.

II.

Nothing that nags

No timers, no streaks, no notifications. Our things wait for you. Pick them up when you have the time to think.

III.

Made to keep

Good paper, careful typesetting, ideas that hold up on a second visit. Things worth a place on the shelf.
