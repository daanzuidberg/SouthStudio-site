Legal

Privacy policy

SouthStudio respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you subscribe to our dispatch or pre-order Timeline, why we collect it, and the rights you have over your data.

Who we are

SouthStudio is an independent studio. For any privacy question, email info@southstudio.online.

What we collect

Newsletter: your email address. Pre-orders: your name, email address and the edition you chose. We never ask for more than we need, and we don't collect payment details on this site.

Why we collect it & legal basis

We use your details only to send the dispatch you asked for, or to reserve and later fulfil your pre-order. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed for that purpose, until you unsubscribe, or until your pre-order is fulfilled or cancelled.

Who we share it with

Only the service that delivers our email and forms on our behalf. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time, every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and you can reach us at info@southstudio.online. You may also contact your data protection authority.

Cookies & analytics

This site stores a little information in your browser to remember your game progress and language preference. We keep tracking to a minimum.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Last updated: June 2026.
