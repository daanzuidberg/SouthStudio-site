The Studio

An independent studio.
Made by hand.

SouthStudio makes things worth thinking about — games, books, and objects. Each one is rigorous underneath, beautiful in the hand, and made to keep.

The name

Zuid·berg.
South·mountain.

SouthStudio comes from Zuidberg — the family name behind everything here. Zuid, south. Berg, mountain. A direction and a horizon: the place you set out toward, the long way round rather than the quick win.

It keeps the studio personal. Everything here is made by someone, not assembled by a committee — and that is the whole point.

Etymology
Zuid → South
berg → Mountain
→ A direction and a climb
Est. 2026

Founded in the Netherlands. Built for anyone in the world who likes to use their mind.

The standard

Five things every
release must be.

Before anything leaves the studio, it has to pass all five. If it fails one, it isn't ready — whatever the format.

01

Honest

There is always a fair path through. Nothing cheap, nothing arbitrary — the answer is earned.

02

Beautiful

Typography, materials, and detail considered to the edge. Worth keeping on a shelf.

03

Deeper than it looks

A world underneath, not just a mechanic. It rewards a second and third visit.

04

Made to last

Physical or digital, it is built to be returned to — not consumed and forgotten.

05

Respectful

Of your time, your attention, and your intelligence. We talk to a peer.
