Your first day, Investigator.

Reality is fraying. Somewhere upstream, history was altered, and the damage propagates forward into your present. The Agency does not change the past, we restore it.

Each case is a deduction grid. Read the evidence, reconstruct what truly happened, and identify the keystone: the one anomaly that, removed, lets the timeline heal.

Mark what is impossible. Confirm what is certain. Eliminate down to the only configuration the evidence allows. Every case here resolves to exactly one answer.

The deeper your clearance, the less you should trust the records. At Specialist level and beyond, one item of evidence in each file is corrupted, flagged UNSTABLE. It remembers something that never happened. Discard it.

CASE 01

Gears in the Pyramid

Giza, Lower Egypt·2579 BCE to 2510 BCE

The survey team broke through a false wall expecting grave goods. Instead they found a brass mechanism, still ticking, sealed in bone-dry air for forty-five centuries. By every law of history it should not be invented for another four thousand years.

Four anachronistic parts were carried into the dynasty across four reigns, each logged to a different official and a different quarry. The papyrus that lets you place them has survived, barely; the rest is yours to deduce.

File the complete record: 4 years set against Figure, Artifact. No entry repeats. Mark the grid from the evidence below until a single timeline holds together.
