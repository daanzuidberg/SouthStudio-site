## 1 · What SouthStudio is

SouthStudio is an **independent studio making things worth thinking about** —
games, books, and objects. Each one is rigorous underneath, beautiful in the
hand, and deeper than it first appears.

We are **not** a puzzle press, an app shop, or a publisher. Those are formats.
SouthStudio is the *sensibility* behind them — the quiet mark that tells you a
thing was made with care, a point of view, and respect for the person on the
other end.

- **Founded:** 2026, independent.
- **Made by someone**, not assembled by a committee. Everything is personal — that's the whole point.
- **For the long way round** rather than the quick win.

## 2 · Where the name comes from

*South* comes from **Zuidberg** — the family name behind the studio.
*Zuid* = south; *berg* = mountain.

> Zuid → South · berg → mountain · **→ a direction, and a climb.**

A direction and a horizon: the place you set out toward, the long way round.
It keeps the studio personal and human.

## 4 · The standard every product must meet

Before anything leaves the studio it must pass **all five**. Fail one and it
isn't ready — whatever the format.

1. **Honest** — There is always a fair path through. Nothing cheap, nothing arbitrary; the answer is earned.
2. **Beautiful** — Typography, materials, and detail considered to the edge. Worth keeping on a shelf.
3. **Deeper than it looks** — A world underneath, not just a mechanic. Rewards a second and third visit.
4. **Made to last** — Physical or digital, built to be returned to — not consumed and forgotten.
5. **Respectful** — Of your time, your attention, and your intelligence. We talk to a peer.

## 5 · How we sound

- **Confident, not loud** — We state things plainly. We don't shout, hype, or pad.
- **Witty, never gimmicky** — A dry turn of phrase, yes. A pun in a headline, no.
- **Precise** — We choose words the way we design grids: every one earns its place.
- **Peer to peer** — We never talk down to a beginner or up to an expert. Just across.

**House style.** We write *"things worth thinking about,"* never *"premium
brain-training."* We say what a thing **is** and trust the reader to want it.
The TIMELINE dispatch voice — wry, in-world, exact — is the reference.

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| "Things worth thinking about." | "Premium brain-training games." |
| "80 deduction cases, one provable answer each." | "Hundreds of fun brain teasers!!" |
| "Made to keep." | "Download now — limited time!" |
| "History has been edited. You are the correction." | "Can YOU solve it?!" |

## 6 · Visual identity

### The mark
A **logic grid** — one red cell, one black, the rest open. It is literally the
thing we make: the mechanic *as* the identity. Use it as the studio signature on
everything, small and quiet. **Never redraw it.**

Grid layout (3×3, reading order): `outline · RED · outline / outline · outline · INK / INK · outline · outline`

### Wordmark
**South**Studio — "Studio" set in the red. Often paired with the kicker
*"Independent Studio"* in mono caps.

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#1A1612` | Primary text, dark surfaces |
| **Ink 2** | `#46413A` | Body text |
| **Dim** | `#6B6256` | Secondary / mono labels |
| **Red** | `#D2362B` | The one accent — mark, links, emphasis. Use sparingly. |
| **Paper** | `#FAF7EF` | Primary background |
| **Paper 2** | `#F1EBDD` | Cards, panels, insets |
| **Line** | `#DDD4C2` | Hairlines |
| **Line 2** | `#C8BDA6` | Stronger borders |

Warm paper + near-black ink + a single confident red. No gradients, no second accent.

### Typography
- **Serif — Newsreader** → headlines & body. The studio's voice.
- **Mono — IBM Plex Mono** → labels, system text, kickers, captions (uppercase, wide letter-spacing).

> **Type note.** The live website currently still mixes in **Spectral** (its
> footers read "Set in Spectral & IBM Plex Mono"). The brand direction is to
> **consolidate on Newsreader** across studio + products so everything reads as
> one hand. Treat Newsreader as canonical; Spectral is legacy to be phased out.

### Visual tics
- Numbered sections and catalog entries (`No. 001`, `01 / …`).
- Thin frames with small red corner brackets.
- Dossier / field-manual styling — in-world "Time Correction Agency" framing for Timeline.

## 8 · Flagship — No. 001, *Timeline*

**The pitch:** *History has been edited. You are the correction.*

A book of **80 unique logic-deduction cases** set inside the **Time Correction
Agency**. You're a new field investigator: each case is a small paradox in the
historical record — a date that doesn't add up, a witness who can't have been
there, an object that shouldn't exist yet — and a chain of reasoning that puts it
right.

**Principles in the product**
- **Solvable** — every case has exactly one answer, reachable by logic alone. No trivia, no luck.
- **Story-led** — each puzzle is a tiny mystery, not a grid to grind.
- **Five clearances** — difficulty ladder from coffee-break warm-up to genuine head-scratcher.
- **Fully worked** — step-by-step solutions and reasoning notes at the back.

**Two ways to play**
- **The Book** — Smyth-sewn, lies-flat binding · ~200 pages · warm uncoated pencil-friendly stock · 80 unique cases, only in print.
- **Play Online (free)** — a *different* set of cases in the browser; instant checking, one-at-a-time hints, auto-save, a fresh free case weekly. A taste before the book. (Live build: ~30 cases across three tiers.)

**Editions & pricing**
- Standard — **€24**
- Signed first edition — **€29**
- Book + online, gift-wrapped — **€32**
- Ships **Q4 2026**. Pre-orders: no charge until it ships.
