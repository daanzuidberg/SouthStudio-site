The concept

The Correction

A cinematic 35-second TikTok. We never show a person's face — only hands, objects, atmosphere. Voice-over carries the narrative; visuals carry the world. The hook drops the viewer into the TCA fiction before they know it's a book ad. By the time they realise, they already want in.

The tone is quiet, deliberate, slightly ominous. Think: found footage from a government archive. Warm desk-lamp light. Paper. Ink. The satisfying click of a stamp.

Putting it together

01
Generate shots in Higgsfield first — all 7 clips before you open an editor. For shots 2 and 7 (Sora 2), generate 2–3 variants and pick the best. For Kling 3.0 shots, one take is usually enough.
02
Record the voice-over before editing. Import the audio first and cut video to match it — not the other way around. The VO timing in the brief above is the guide, not a hard lock.
03
Music: atmospheric, slow, no heavy beat. Search "dark ambient" or "study lo-fi" on TikTok's sound library. The music should sit under the voice-over, not compete. Keep it at ~20% volume.
04
Text cards on shot 7: add three overlays in sequence — TIMELINE. (large serif, centre) → southstudio.online (small mono, lower third) → link in bio → (mono, smallest). Use the SouthStudio palette: paper text on ink background, or ink text on paper background.
05
Colour grade: all clips should feel like one piece of film. In CapCut, apply a warm film LUT (try "Film 03" or "Kodak") at 40–60% opacity. Slightly lower the highlights, lift the shadows — the warmth and darkness should feel consistent across all 7 shots.
06
TikTok caption: "80 logic puzzles set in a fictional government archive. Which clearance level can you reach? 🔴 Pre-order link in bio. #puzzles #logicpuzzles #booklaunch #indiegames #deduction"
